# Audit export for members and funders

## Description

Lets permitted members and funders export an audit bundle of events with their proofs and hashes, which a stand-alone verifier can check without the app.

As a member or funder,  
I want an exportable audit of a community or an order,  
So that I can check it independently.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a permitted request
   **When** I export
   **Then** I receive a bundle of events with their proofs and hashes that a stand-alone verifier can check without the app.
