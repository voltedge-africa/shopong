# Arrange delivery to the shared collection point

## Description

A treasurer plans delivery of a paid order to the chosen collection point, assigning a route, an ETA and a driver, and members are notified of the window.

As a treasurer,  
I want delivery planned to our chosen collection point,  
So that everyone knows when and where.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a paid order and a collection point
   **When** I plan delivery
   **Then** a route, ETA and driver are assigned and members are notified of the window.
