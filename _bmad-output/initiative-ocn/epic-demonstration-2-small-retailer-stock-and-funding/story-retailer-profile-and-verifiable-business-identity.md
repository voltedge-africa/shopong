# Retailer profile and verifiable business identity

## Description

A small retailer creates a business profile signed by their DID that shows only the fields they choose to reveal, and a supplier circle can invite them with a capability.

As a small retailer,  
I want a profile that others can verify,  
So that suppliers and funders can trust me.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** my identity
   **When** I create a business profile
   **Then** it is signed by my DID and shows what I choose to reveal
   **And** a supplier circle can invite me with a capability.
