# Track and repay obligations

## Description

A retailer sees what they owe, when and to whom, and repays through a Stitch pay-in, with a settled payment closing the obligation on both sides.

As a retailer,  
I want to see what I owe and when,  
So that I stay in good standing.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** open obligations
   **When** I open them
   **Then** I see amount, due date and counterparty and can repay by Stitch pay-in
   **And** a settled payment closes the obligation on both sides.
