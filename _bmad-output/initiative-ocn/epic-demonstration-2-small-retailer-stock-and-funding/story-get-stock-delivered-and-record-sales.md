# Get stock delivered and record sales

## Description

Confirmed delivery of a funded order adds the stock to the retailer's record, and simple sales events are recorded with totals shown.

As a retailer,  
I want stock delivered and my sales recorded,  
So that I can repay and show my track record.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a funded order
   **When** delivery is confirmed
   **Then** stock is added to my record
   **And** I can record sales events simply, with totals shown.
